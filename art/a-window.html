A Window

An interactive story about my memory during Covid with p5.js

View Project →

‍UCLA Design Media Arts "Fill in the blank, I am ___" Undergraduate Exhibition Award 2022
‍

During the pandemic, I spent a lot of time standing in front of the window and looking at the photo album to think of past moments.

Windows were portals that took me back to some place at some time. However, I sometimes forgot the time when I was lost in memories. They were just photos with my imagination in a cycle.

The interactive work traces my feelings when I recall good memories by looking outside from the window.
