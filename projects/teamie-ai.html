Overview

Introduction

Teamie AI provides a streamlined experience of training chatbot and integrates into daily workflow for businesses and teams, powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) like OpenAI GPT-4.

Problem

Data reveals that an average employee spends approximately 30% of their workday searching for information. Interacting with various departments for support, from computer troubleshooting to payroll queries, is often time-consuming and daunting, especially for new employees. The absence of a centralized knowledge base posed significant efficiency challenges.

Goal

The primary goal is to revolutionize access to internal knowledge through AI-powered chatbots. The design focuses on making the process less technical, more intuitive, and time-efficient, enabling even non-technical users to leverage AI capabilities.

SOLUTION

Final Outcome

Create Chatbot from knowledge base with Model Preferences

The level of customization ensures the chatbot can accurately respond to specific inquiries related to the uploaded content, making it a powerful for each unique team within an organization.

Manage knowledge base as bussiness evolves

The chatbot stays updated with the company's information through a clearly structured knowledge base.

Chat with Trained bots

The bots are trained on the company's knowledge base and can answer a wide range of queries instantly.

Manage Team Access to the Chatbots

To ensure seamless collaboration, Teamie enables leaders to manage access to the chatbots and knowledge bases.

Design Research

Observations

We initiated a user research through interviews and surveys with a range of potential users, including team leads, IT managers, and new employees. Our research revealed that over 80% employees felt overwhelmed by the amount of scattered information and needed a more streamlined way to access relevant data.

"How might we simplify and streamline the process of accessing internal knowledge to increase efficiency through generative AI technology?"

IDEATION

Design Goal

The primary goal is to develop a product that leveraged the power of generative language models in a user-friendly manner. The chat interface needs to feel intuitive and familiar to users, minimizing the learning curve. The trained model should deliver accurate responses to meet business requirements.

Information Architecture

DEsign

User Interface Design

The final product features a clean, user-friendly interface and a powerful AI that could efficiently process and deliver information from various sources within a team.
